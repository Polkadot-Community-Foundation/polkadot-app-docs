# Get started as a user

The Polkadot app holds your account on your own device. With it you can claim a
username, top up and send CASH, chat with your contacts and open apps that
people have built for the Polkadot Products Devnet.

The app is self-custodial. Your keys are created and stored on your device, and
nothing is signed until you approve it.

!!! warning "Never enter a real seed phrase"
    Never type a recovery phrase that holds real value into a devnet build, and
    never share your recovery phrase with anyone.

## Install the app

--8<-- "install-app.md"

If you only want to open a few apps, you can skip the install and use the web
gateway at [dev-dot.li](https://dev-dot.li) in any browser. For an on-device
account, payments and signing, install one of the apps above.

## What to do next

Work through these guides in order:

1. [Create an account & get funds](../guides/create-account.md). Create or
   import an account and fund it.
2. [Back up & restore your account](../guides/back-up-your-account.md). Save
   the recovery phrase and turn on the cloud backup before you need them.
3. [Get & use CASH](../guides/get-and-use-cash.md). Top up your balance and
   send some to someone.
4. [Username & proof of personhood](../guides/username-and-personhood.md).
   Claim a readable name to use instead of a long address.
5. [Discover & open apps](../guides/discover-and-open-apps.md). Find apps in
   Browse and open them by their `.dot` domain.
6. [Messaging & calls](../guides/messaging-and-calls.md). Chat with and call
   your contacts.
